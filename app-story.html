Even Building an App Can Test Your Patience! 😂

23 November 2026 · SSAZA FM 99.8

There is plenty that happens behind the scenes before a radio station can bring a new digital experience to its listeners. At SSAZA FM 99.8, our Android app project has given us a few memorable moments — and a good reason to laugh along the way!

Our work has taken us through app design, GitHub files, Android installation packages, streaming connections and plenty of testing. Sometimes a page opens when we expect a player; sometimes an installation prompt appears again; and sometimes one small setting sends us back to check everything from the beginning.

After a long troubleshooting session, the joke was that the app had made its developer look tired — and that we had even started making errors from exhaustion. 😂

“Even building an app can test your patience — but we are getting there, one step at a time!”

Behind the laughter is a serious goal: to make it easier for our listeners to enjoy SSAZA FM 99.8, stay connected with the station and access more of our content through their phones.

We are taking the process step by step, checking the details and working towards a smooth, reliable experience. We appreciate everyone who continues to support the station as we grow beyond the airwaves.

SSAZA FM 99.8 — your voice, your music, your station.
